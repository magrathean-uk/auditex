# M03 explainer-training
Track B, same rules as M01. Ends at a judged build with a recommendation; whether a model explainer enters a product release, and how, is a later G gate. The Hugging Face upload stays parked until a build passes its bar and G says yes. No M03 run starts before the budget look.

## Goal
G has looked at the budget against the allocation balance before any M03 run; the candidate bases are compared on the explainer job with a measured noise band and EU-first decides inside it; one data round aimed at what the judge rewards passes a validation-only pilot before any training data is regenerated (DEC-037); the large runs on the chosen base train on that data and select only on validation; one judged build is scored once on the clean held-out set; and G picks the final build (or none) from a written recommendation that lists the product-integration options.
